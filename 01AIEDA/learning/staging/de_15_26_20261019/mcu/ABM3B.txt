The ABM3B series is a quartz crystal offered in a 5.0mm x 3.2mm x 1.1mm four-pad SMD package. 
Tight frequency accuracy of ±10ppm and stability of ±15ppm over operating temperature range of 
-40°C to +85°C, low plating load (CL) value of 6pF, and low Equivalent Series Resistance (ESR) is 
achieved in this compact package. The ABM3B series offers industry standard frequencies 
common for communication, test equipment, high density, PCMCIA end applications.
